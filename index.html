FibroPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

FibroPulse is a symptom and trigger tracker for people living with fibromyalgia. It helps you record how you feel each day and look for possible links between what you record and your symptoms or flares. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. We have written it in plain language on purpose.

1. Who is responsible for your data

FibroPulse is developed and operated by Gérard du Plessis (the "controller" of your data). You can contact us about anything in this policy at gerard.duplessis@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Some of this is health information, which is sensitive, so we ask for your explicit consent before you create an account.

InformationWhy we need it
Your email address and password (the password is stored in protected, hashed form and we cannot read it)To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
Profile details you choose to give: sex, age range, reproductive status, other health conditions, time since diagnosis, and your diet and diet changesTo personalise the app (for example, showing the menstrual cycle question only when relevant), to add context to your doctor report, and to look for patterns linked to diet changes. All of these are optional.
Your daily check-ins: symptoms (such as pain, fatigue, brain fog, mood), flare days, sleep, stress, exertion, digestive symptoms, food and drink, relief strategies used, notable events, and any notesThis is the core of the app. It is what the pattern analysis works from.
Your medications: names, doses, frequencies, start and stop dates, and whether you took them each dayTo track medication and to look for links between medication and your symptoms.
Daily weather for your area (temperature, air pressure, humidity, rain)To look for possible links between weather and your symptoms.
The patterns the app calculates from your recordsTo show them to you on the Insights page and in your doctor report.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

3. Weather and approximate location

To fetch the weather for a check-in, the app asks your phone for your approximate location. The location is rounded to roughly an 11 km area, is sent to the free weather service Open-Meteo to get that day's weather, and is not saved by us. If you decline location permission, the check-in still saves, only without weather data.

4. Medicine box scanning

If you use the option to scan a medicine box, the photo is read on your own phone to pull out the name, strength and frequency. The photo is not uploaded and is not kept.

5. Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone and the chosen time is stored on your phone. The notification text is generic and never contains any of your health information.

6. Where your data is stored and who can see it

  • Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union).
  • It is protected in transit with encryption, and each account can only reach its own records.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.
  • The only outside services involved are Supabase (storing and processing your data on our behalf) and Open-Meteo (receiving the rounded location described above).

7. How we use your information

We use it only to run FibroPulse for you: to show your history, calculate possible patterns between what you record and your symptoms, and produce your doctor report. We do not use it to make decisions about you, and the app does not diagnose or give medical advice.

We do not currently use your data for research or share it in any form. If we ever wish to use information (for example, in combined, anonymised form) for research, we will ask for your separate, explicit consent first, and you can say no without losing any part of the app.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything linked to it is permanently deleted.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: in the app, go to Settings and choose Download CSV or Download JSON.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app, go to Settings and choose Delete my account. This is permanent.
  • Correct your information: you can edit your profile, medications and check-ins in the app at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop at any time by deleting your account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority (for example, the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, your national authority in the EU, or the Information Regulator in South Africa). To use any of these rights, contact us at the email above.

10. Children

FibroPulse is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Not medical advice

FibroPulse shows possible links in your own records. These are not proof of cause and not a diagnosis. Please talk to your doctor or care team before making any change to your treatment.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
